# 🛒 Online Retail Insights: Sales Trends and Customer Analysis

---

## 📘 Project Overview

This project focuses on **exploratory data analysis (EDA)** and **insight generation** from the **Online Retail II dataset**.  
The objective is to analyze **sales trends**, **customer behavior**, and **transactional patterns** to derive meaningful business insights that can support **data-driven decision-making** in a retail environment.

The analysis is implemented in a **Jupyter Notebook** and follows a structured data analytics workflow suitable for **academic submission**, **portfolio presentation**, and **business analytics case studies**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand the structure and characteristics of retail transaction data  
- Analyze **sales performance over time**  
- Identify **top-selling products** and **high-value customers**  
- Examine **customer purchasing behavior**  
- Generate actionable business insights from historical sales data  

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Information

- **Dataset Name**: Online Retail II (UCI)  
- **Source**: Kaggle  
- **Dataset Link**:  
  🔗 [Online Retail II (UCI)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mashlyn/online-retail-ii-uci)

### 📊 Key Attributes
- Invoice number  
- Stock code  
- Product description  
- Quantity  
- Invoice date  
- Unit price  
- Customer ID  
- Country  

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The following analyses were performed:

- Data structure and summary statistics  
- Missing value and duplicate analysis  
- Sales distribution analysis  
- Time-based sales trends (daily, monthly)  
- Country-wise sales performance  
- Customer-level purchase analysis  

---

## ⚙️ Data Preprocessing

- Removed cancelled and invalid transactions  
- Handled missing customer IDs  
- Converted invoice date to datetime format  
- Created derived features such as:
  - Total sales value
  - Month and year
  - Transaction-level metrics  

---

## 📈 Key Analyses Performed

- **Sales Trend Analysis**  
  - Monthly and yearly revenue patterns  
- **Product Analysis**  
  - Best-selling products by quantity and revenue  
- **Customer Analysis**  
  - Identification of repeat and high-value customers  
- **Geographical Analysis**  
  - Sales contribution by country  

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

- Sales exhibit **seasonal patterns**, with noticeable monthly fluctuations  
- A small percentage of customers contribute significantly to total revenue  
- Certain products consistently dominate sales volume  
- Majority of revenue originates from a limited number of countries
